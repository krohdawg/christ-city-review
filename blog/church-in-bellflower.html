Looking for a Church in Bellflower? Christ City House Churches Are Coming

If you live in Bellflower and are looking for a church, a Christ City house church for neighbors in Bellflower is coming.

A church that meets in homes and on Sundays

Christ City is one church family who gathers on Sundays and shares life in house churches throughout the week. Sundays meet in La Mirada at Heights Christian Schools, Bluefield Campus, 12900 Bluefield Ave. Weekly Sunday gatherings begin on Launch Sunday, March 7, 2027.

What happens in a house church

We gather in homes to study Scripture, share meals, pray, care for one another, and love our neighbors. These everyday practices help us center our lives on Christ and put our faith into action. Read the simple guide to house churches.

Our hope for every city

Our hope is to see communities of followers of Jesus taking root wherever our people live, centering their lives on Christ and pursuing people with love.

How to hear when it starts

Leave your name on our Bellflower page and we will tell you when it meets. Want to open your home? Get in touch.
