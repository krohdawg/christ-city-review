A Church Plant in La Mirada: The Road to Launch Sunday

Christ City Church is a church plant: a brand new church starting in La Mirada, California. Co-pastors Kenny and Kim Baker are planting it side by side, and Launch Sunday is March 7, 2027.

The road to launch

  • December 20, 2026: Christmas at Christ City, our first preview service
  • January 10, 17, 24 and 31: Discover Christ City
  • February 7: Public preview service
  • February 14, 21 and 28: Bible studies
  • March 7: Launch Sunday, kicking off our four week Grand Opening series. Weekly Sunday gatherings begin.
  • March 28: Easter at Christ City

All of these meet at Heights Christian Schools, Bluefield Campus, 12900 Bluefield Ave, La Mirada. Times are on our Coming up page.

Why we are starting

Our vision is to see our city renewed by Christ through people who live like Jesus. Our mission is to help people center their lives on Christ and join him in pursuing people with love.

How to help

Every gift goes toward opening a church for our neighbors, one house at a time. You can give once or monthly, join the prayer team, or get updates as we get closer.
